# Playback

Choosing a stream opens a full-screen player that shows only the video. Select, up or down shows the controls: title, a focusable progress bar, elapsed and remaining time, back/forward one skip interval (10 s by default, Settings › Playback), play/pause, and buttons for subtitles, audio and speed that open a panel of choices above them, focused on the current one. The controls hide after 5 s of playing, never while paused, scanning or a panel is open. On the subtitles panel each add-on is a row that opens a page of its files. Menu ends a scan, goes back from an add-on page, then closes the panel, then the controls, then the player. Closing a panel puts focus straight back on its button. With subtitles on, the subtitles panel starts with Timing, which opens a strip along the bottom for moving the subtitles earlier or later while the video plays. Below the buttons sit the names of the content tabs (Info, Episodes for a series, Chapters when the file has them); down opens them below the transport bar while the video plays on.

## Sub-features

- `play-open` starts the stream; with a saved position it starts there.
- `play-controls` shows the controls with focus on play/pause.
- `play-speed` changes the playback speed from the Speed panel.
- `play-subtitles` lists embedded tracks and selects one; the app draws its cues over the video, text and bitmap (PGS) alike.
- `play-seek` skips one skip interval (10 s by default) with left and right while the controls are hidden, and shows the controls for 2 s with the progress bar focused.
- `play-subtitle-timing` moves the subtitles: left and right 0.1 s earlier or later as they go down, held 0.5 s at a time after that, select starts the highlighted line (the nearest to the playhead) now, Menu leaves; the delay is remembered with the subtitles for the same stream.
- `play-speech-timing` shows on the timing strip where people speak above the subtitles, and offers the delay that lines them up; up applies it.
- `play-timeline` on the focused progress bar: every tap jumps one skip interval, quick taps in one direction add up beside the picture ("+30s"), a hold scans at 2× and further presses change the rate.
- `play-markers` draws the stream's chapters as gaps in the progress bar and its skip segments (intro, recap, credits, preview) as warm tinted spans; with the bar focused or scanning, the chapter at the playhead is named above it in place of the title.
- `play-tabs` shows Info, Episodes and Chapters as names in the transport bar's row of buttons, between play/pause and the track buttons: focus crosses them without opening anything; selecting one opens its tab below the bar and moves focus to what plays now in it. With a tab open, left and right across the names switch tabs, reaching any other button closes it, down from a name lands on what plays now, up goes back to the open tab's name, and Menu from anywhere in the tabs returns to play/pause. Episodes starts at the playing one; choosing another opens its streams over the player, or plays the up next episode at once. Choosing a chapter plays from its start with the progress bar focused. The controls stay up while focus is in the tabs.
- `play-live` plays a live channel (type `tv`): `player.live` (LIVE) replaces the progress bar, the times and back/forward; left and right show the controls on play/pause instead of seeking.
- `play-stop` returns to the screen that opened the stream list: Details, or Home for a hero Play or a Continue Watching card.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- [Find streams](./find-streams.md), then select a stream.

## Driving it with tv.sh

Preconditions:

- A stream list is on screen with focus on a `streams.row`.

- **Open.** `$T select`. Within 30 s `$T text player.status` reads `… · playing · 1920×1080 · libavcodec H264 (SW)`.
- **Controls.** After 5 s the controls are gone (screenshot shows video only). `$T select`; `$T focused` prints `player.playPause`.
- **Speed.** `$T focus player.speed right 6`, `$T select` (focus lands on Normal, `player.option`), press down twice, `$T select`. Reopening the Speed panel focuses 1.5× (labels follow the simulator's locale, such as `1,5×`).
- **Subtitles.** `$T focus player.subtitles left 3`, `$T select`: the panel lists Built-in (Off, the tracks) and Add-ons (one row per subtitles add-on). `$T remote down`, `$T select` picks the first track; the log shows `chooseSubtitles(…builtIn…)`. For a text track, within a few seconds of dialogue `$T text player.subtitle` prints the cue and `$T evidence` shows it above the picture's bottom edge. A bitmap track (`codec=dvd_subtitle` or PGS in the log) has no element: the log's `subtitle-delivery … published=1` marks a cue, and a screenshot then shows it. Off clears it at once.
- **Subtitle timing.** Needs a stream whose link stays the same across plays, so the remembered delay can be checked: the fixture source (`scripts/dev-sources.sh up`, `$T launch -STREMIO_FIXTURE_SOURCE 1`), Home › Fixture Movies › Sintel › Play, the `streams.row` labeled `Fixture, Sintel (2010).mkv`. On the subtitles panel open the Fixture row and pick `English 1`, its sidecar: one cue from 0:01 to 0:30 ("A subtitle from a sidecar file."). Within its first 15 s: `$T focus player.subtitles.timing up 6` (label `Timing, In Sync`), `$T select`. `$T focused` prints `player.timing` and the strip shows the cue as a block under the playhead. `$T remote right` three times: `$T text player.timing.offset` reads `0,3 s Later` and a screenshot shows the block moved off its dashed outline. Hold with `agent-device tv-remote press right --duration-ms 1600 --platform ios --target tv --session stremio-tv-verify`: 0.1 s at once and 2.5 s more (`2,9 s Later`). Within 10 s of the cue's start the block has a bright border and the hint reads "This line starts now" (further away, "No line nearby"); `$T select` sets the delay so it starts now (at 0:08 about `6,9 s Later`). `$T remote menu` leaves the strip for the bare video; reopening the panel shows `Timing, 6,9 s Later` and Reset Timing, and `source build/verify/current.env; grep -o '"subtitleDelay":[-0-9]*' "$storage/streams.json"` prints `"subtitleDelay":6900`. To check it is remembered, relaunch with the fixture still listed (`$T relaunch` drops the launch option, so run `agent-device open com.ograbek.takane … --relaunch` with the storage, mute and fixture `--launch-args`), play the same `streams.row` from Continue Watching, and the panel reads `Timing, 6,9 s Later`. Reset Timing reads `Timing, In Sync` again.
- **Speech lane and suggestion.** The fixture clips are silent, so use streams with speech. For the lane: Home › Sintel (`poster.tt1727587`) › Play, the `streams.row` labeled `Open Movies, 1080p · CC BY 3.0 · Blender Foundation`, which keeps its embedded English subtitles on. Open the strip as above: the Speech row has light blocks where people speak, lined up with the cue blocks below, a thin line where audio played without speech, and dots ahead of the playhead, where nothing has played yet. For the suggestion, Breaking Bad S5 E6 as in Markers: the app picks an OpenSubtitles file that runs about 25 s late against the file's own English track. Scan to about 11:30 (hold right, then right four times for 32×, select to play), and wait about 90 s with the strip closed: the log has `hearing speech off` once the suggestion is found. Open the strip: `$T text player.timing.suggestion` reads about `Suggested: 25,3 s Earlier. Press Up to apply` at once, and the log has `hearing speech on` until Menu closes it. `$T remote up`: `player.timing.offset` reads that delay, the lines match the speech lane, and the suggestion goes. Press right 20 times (`2,0 s` more): it comes back about 2 s earlier. On the file's own English (Full) track no suggestion shows. The log has `audio tap installed (backend=software live=true)` once the stream plays, muted included.
- **Timeline.** Controls showing, `$T focus player.progress up 3`, then right away `$T remote right` three times: `player.status` jumps about 30 s at the default interval, and a screenshot within a second (`xcrun simctl io <udid> screenshot`) shows "+30s" at the right edge. With the controls hidden, one `$T remote right` jumps 10 s and `$T focused` prints `player.progress`; 2 s later the controls are gone. Hold with `agent-device tv-remote press right --duration-ms 800 --platform ios --target tv --session stremio-tv-verify`: the phase reads `paused`, the time keeps advancing about 2 s per second, and the snapshot has a `2×` label. `$T remote right` makes it `4×`; `$T remote left` twice reads `playing` again. Hold again and `$T select` (or `$T remote menu`): `playing`, still in the player.
- **Markers.** Needs a file with chapters and an episode with skip data. Breaking Bad S5 E6 from the debrid add-on has both: its file has chapters titled with their start times (shown as `Chapter 1`…), and IntroDB has its intro (3:51 to 4:08) and credits. Search `breaking bad`, `poster.tt0903747`, walk down to the Season 5 row, `$T focus episode.tt0903747:5:6 right 5`, select, the first `streams.row`. Pause with the controls up: the bar has gaps (the second chapter starts at the intro's end) and a warm span at about 8 % and at the very end. `$T focus player.progress up 3`: "Chapter 1" replaces the title above the bar, and the raw snapshot's `player.progress` has `"value":"Chapter 1"`. Hold right (`agent-device tv-remote press right --duration-ms 800 …`) and press right four times for 32×: past 4:08 the name moves with the playhead and reads `Chapter 2`. For named chapters on a stream you control, give the fixture a chapter file (`ffmpeg -i in.mkv -i chapters.txt -map 0 -map_chapters 1 -c copy out.mkv`, `;FFMETADATA1` with `[CHAPTER]` blocks) and restore the original afterwards.
- **Tabs.** Breaking Bad S5 E6 as in Markers (a series, 6 chapters). Controls showing, `$T focus player.tab.info right 4`: `$T text player.info` finds nothing (focus alone opens no tab). `$T remote right` prints `player.tab.episodes`; `$T select` opens the Season 5 shelf below, starting at episode 6, and `$T focused` prints `player.episode.tt0903747:5:6`. Wait 8 s: still focused (no autohide). `$T remote up` prints `player.tab.episodes`; `$T remote right` prints `player.tab.chapters` and Chapters replaces the shelf; `$T remote down` prints the playing chapter (`player.chapter.0` in the first 4 minutes). `$T remote up`, then `$T remote left` twice: `player.tab.info` with `$T text player.info` starting `Breaking Bad, Season 5, Episode 6 · Buyout`; one more `$T remote left` focuses Forward and closes the tab. Back in Chapters, `$T remote right`, `$T select`: `player.status` reads about `0:04:10`, `$T focused` prints `player.progress` and its raw `"value"` is `Chapter 2`. From a card or a name, `$T remote menu` prints `player.playPause`; a second Menu hides the controls. In Episodes, choosing episode 1 opens its `streams.row`s full screen over the player and Menu there returns focus to `player.episode.tt0903747:5:1`; choosing a stream plays it (`· S5 E1`, controls hidden). Choosing episode 2 from there plays it at once through Up Next (the log has one `"NextVideo"`). With Settings › Playback › Hide Spoilers on and episode 3 marked watched on Details, the Episodes tab blurs the unwatched stills and leaves out their overviews, keeps episode 3 (`Watched`) and the playing one clear.
- **Live.** Needs the free Pluto TV add-on: install `https://dev.nebulawp.org/stremio/pluto-tv-addon/manifest.json` as in [Install an add-on](./install-addon.md) (`$T type addons.manifestURL down <url>`; 7 installed). Home gains "Pluto TV Channels" and "Pluto TV Popular Channels" rows about six presses down from the tab bar; posters are `poster.<channel slug>`, such as `poster.cnn-headlines`. Select one; Details offers `Play` (not Play First Episode); select it, then the first `streams.row`. Within 30 s `player.status` reads `0:00:10 / 0:00:00 · playing · 1280×720` (no decoder field; the time counts up from the start, the duration stays 0:00:00). `$T remote right`: `$T focused` prints `player.playPause`, `$T text player.live` prints `Live` and `$T text player.progress` finds nothing. Menu closes the controls, then returns to Details.
- **Stop.** `$T remote menu` until Details is back with Resume (closes the controls, then the player). The stream list closed once the stream started playing; a stream that fails returns to it.

## Gotchas

- agent-device's tvOS remote has no play/pause button; use the controls' play/pause.
- The simulator decodes in software (`libavcodec … (SW)`). Hardware decoding needs a real Apple TV.
- Large REMUX streams take 5-15 s to start.
- The controls hide 5 s after the last press while playing, and arrows then skip one interval on the bare video instead. Each `$T focus` step reads a snapshot (about 1 s), so a long focus walk can outlast them. Pause first (select on `player.playPause`): the controls then stay up.
- The session picks a subtitle track in the profile's language at load, so subtitles may already show before the panel is opened.
- The player keeps the speed and tracks chosen for a title, so the next stream of it starts at that speed.
- Core remembers a subtitle delay only for the same stream. AIOStreams answers each lookup with new playback links, so a debrid stream played again starts in sync; use the fixture source to check the remembered delay.
- The fixture clip is 90 s and its one cue ends at 0:30 (later with a delay): seek back with the progress bar before opening the strip.
- Skip Intro needs an episode with data in the source Settings › Playback › Skip Data names (Stremio Premium, IntroDB or SkipDB). Breaking Bad S5 E6 has IntroDB data (see Markers).
- The app hears speech only while subtitles show, until it finds a suggestion for that track, and while the timing strip is open; the log has `hearing speech on` and `hearing speech off` as that changes. The lane starts empty at each load and keeps dots where nothing was heard, such as after hearing stopped. The tap runs 3 s ahead on the simulator's software route. A suggestion needs a minute of audio heard where the subtitles have lines, so a quiet scene or a fresh seek shows none.
